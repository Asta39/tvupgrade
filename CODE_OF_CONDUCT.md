# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone who takes part in this project, whether opening issues, sending pull requests, testing on their TV or
answering questions, should be able to do so without harassment, whatever their age, body, ability, ethnicity,
gender identity and expression, experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and
orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Most people asking for help are stuck on a TV that won't do what they want.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no to a change, and explain why when you say no to someone else.
- Keep other people's TVs safe: don't post debloat lists or commands you haven't tested, and say so when you haven't.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, personal attacks, and sexualised language or imagery.
- Trolling, deliberately derailing threads, or harassment in public or private.
- Publishing someone's private information (addresses, network details, keys, door codes) without their permission.
- Anything else a reasonable person would consider unwelcome in a friendly technical project.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainer, [@Asta39](https://github.com/Asta39), through a direct message on GitHub, or
open a [private security advisory](https://github.com/Asta39/tvupgrade/security/advisories/new) if it involves
someone's private data. Reports are kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may
temporarily or permanently block someone from the project. Usually that starts with a private warning.
