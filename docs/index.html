Liquid glass clock

Time you can
see through.

Tall condensed digits, stretched like the iPhone lock screen, work as clear lenses over the wallpaper. The picture bends at every edge, light splits into red and blue like real glass, and the mountain still stands in front of the time.

Nine 4K wallpapers

One button.
A whole new view.

Forests, lakes, tea hills, sunsets. Press CH+ to change scenery. Every one has its own depth cut-out, so the landscape always wraps around the clock.

Everything in its place

Small touches.
Big difference.

Now Playing

While music plays, a frosted mini player sits between the clock and the dock. Press Up and it lights up: OK to pause, Left and Right to skip.

Control Center

Press the gear or the remote’s Menu key. A frosted panel slides in with everything you reach for.

Night Shift

Warms everything on screen, every app and every HDMI input included.

A home for everyone

Up to four profiles, each with their own dock, wallpapers and 3D avatar. Switch in Control Center.

Weather

Now, the next six hours and five days ahead, Apple Weather style.

All your apps

Your favourite eight live in the dock. Press Down for everything else, in clean iOS-style icons.

StandBy

When you’re not watching,
it’s still beautiful.

Six faces on black: huge digits, an analog clock, a calendar, Solar, Float and Now Playing. After 10 minutes idle, StandBy becomes your screensaver, and from 10 pm it turns a dim night red.

Sing along.
Line by line.

Play a song and StandBy shows time-synced lyrics that glide like Apple Music, lit in the album’s most vivid colour. Big artwork, a progress bar and this week’s calendar alongside.

Guest mode

A TV that welcomes
your guests by name.

Switch to Guest mode and the TV greets the people staying, with the Wi-Fi, the weather and only the apps you allow. They scan the code, and their phone becomes the remote. No app, no account, no cloud.

It’s a match.

Can’t agree what to watch? Everyone swipes the same deck on their own phone. When the whole room likes the same title, the TV says so, with a Play button.

🔑

Door code and Wi-Fi

Everything a guest asks on day one is already on their phone: the door code, the dates of the stay and the Wi-Fi password with a Copy button. On the last day a countdown shows how long is left until check-out, so nobody has to message you to ask.

🎶

Fair music queue

Guests search songs on their phones and add them to one shared queue that plays on the TV. Songs take turns between phones, with at most six waiting each, so one person can’t take over the night. Anyone can skip, but you can only remove your own songs. No keys and no accounts.

✨

Arrival show

The first time a guest’s phone opens the page on check-in day, the TV plays a short welcome over your place’s own photos: their names, the Wi-Fi with a code to join from the camera, a tour of the house rules and how the phone works as a remote. It plays once per stay, and you can preview or switch it off in Host settings.

🌤️

Weather by the hour

The phone shows the next six hours with the chance of rain, so guests know whether to take an umbrella. Around it sit sunrise or sunset on a little sun arc, and three ideas for the evening nearby when you add your own AI key.

🍿

Popular on your TV

Posters of what’s trending right now, picked only from the streaming apps this TV actually has. Tap one on the phone and it starts playing on the TV. Up next picks up whatever guests were watching, one tap to resume.

▶️

TizenTube built in

Wherever the launcher opens YouTube, from search, posters, matches or the music queue, it uses TizenTube when it’s installed and falls back to the original YouTube app when it isn’t. Your guests get the ad-free version without having to know it exists.

Debloated

Less clutter. No ads.
Fully undoable.

Sponsored rows, promos and trackers switched off, never uninstalled. No root, no flashing. One script puts everything back.
